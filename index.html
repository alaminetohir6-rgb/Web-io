GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do you work outside Ghana?

Yes. We work worldwide and can discuss your preferred language.

How do I pay?

By MTN MoMo — see “How to pay” above. You can pay from abroad with a supported transfer app.

Can I get changes?

Tell us what to adjust and we will discuss the updates.

Do passport photo orders include printing?

No. Passport and DV Lottery photo services are digital only. You receive JPEG and PDF files; take the file to a photo studio if you need prints.

Can I send school portal passwords on the website?

No. Do not enter passwords or PINs into the website form. If portal access is required, discuss it privately on WhatsApp and only share information you are comfortable sharing.
